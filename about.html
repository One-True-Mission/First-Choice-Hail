Service That Treats You Right

First Choice Auto Hail treats each client with attentiveness and care, providing comprehensive service for you and your vehicle. We save you valuable time and money by running a thorough examination to make sure all of the damage is found and taken care of, not just the obvious dents.

Our goal is to turn a stressful situation into a hassle-free repair experience. We protect your vehicle's value and your investment, working with your insurance and covering deductibles up to $1,000 per vehicle so most repairs come back to you for $0 out of pocket.

When the work is done, your vehicle leaves restored to pre-storm condition. That is the standard every job is held to, every time.
