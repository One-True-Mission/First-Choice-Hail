Our Services

Everything Your Vehicle Needs After The Storm

Hail repair, dent work, and full auto body service, all handled in house and backed by an insurance process that keeps your costs at zero.

What We Offer

Built To Restore Your Vehicle

Whether it is a hood full of hail dents or a single deep ding, we have a repair path that protects your vehicle's value.

⚡

Hail Damage Repair

Hail storms leave dozens of dents across a hood, roof, and panels. We assess the full extent of the damage and restore each panel back to pre-storm condition, working with your insurance so the repair costs you nothing out of pocket.

☼

Paintless Dent Repair

For dings, door dents, and minor damage, paintless dent repair works the metal back into shape from behind the panel. No fillers, no repainting, and your factory finish stays intact.

🔧

Full Auto Body Repair

When damage is too deep for paintless work, we offer a full range of auto body repair. We are equipped to repair any damage done to your vehicle, whether it is from hail or not.

📋

Insurance Claims Help

We work directly with your insurance company and handle the claim for you. We cover deductibles up to $1,000 per vehicle, so most repairs come back to you with $0 out of pocket.

🚚

Pickup & Delivery

We can pick your vehicle up and deliver it back when the work is done. Less time off the road for you, less hassle during an already stressful situation.

🔍

Thorough Inspection

Before any work begins, we run a complete examination of your vehicle to make sure every piece of damage is found and addressed, not just the obvious dents.
